Why we exist

Internships, sized for real life

MicroIntern started from a simple complaint we kept hearing from students: internships ask for a summer, but most students only have a weekend. So we broke the internship down into its smallest useful unit — one task, with a clear brief, a fair rate, and a deadline you can actually see the end of.

For businesses, that same task is often the thing sitting at the bottom of the to-do list: a spreadsheet that needs cleaning, a set of captions that needs writing, a poster that needs designing. MicroIntern gets it in front of someone who wants exactly that kind of work — fast, affordable, and done well.

Our mission is simple: Learn. Apply. Grow. By making internships micro, we make them accessible, joyful, and practical.

For students

Build a portfolio and get paid before you've even graduated.

For businesses

Get small jobs done by someone who's genuinely motivated to do them well.

For everyone

No long contracts. Just clear tasks, fair pay, and quick turnarounds.
